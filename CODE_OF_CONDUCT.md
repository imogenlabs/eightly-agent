# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a friendly, safe, and welcoming environment for all contributors regardless of experience level, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, personal appearance, body size, race, ethnicity, age, religion, nationality, or other similar characteristic.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the project and community
- Showing empathy towards other community members

Examples of unacceptable behavior:
- Harassment, trolling, or deliberately inflammatory comments
- Personal or political attacks
- Publishing others' private information without consent
- Any conduct that would be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to michael@mjashley.com. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated and will result in a response deemed appropriate to the circumstances.
